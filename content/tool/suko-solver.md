Enter the four corner circle totals and the three color-region totals, paint the grid with the matching colors, then hit Solve.

1. Fill in the corner and color totals from your puzzle.
2. Click a color on the left, then click the squares that belong to that color.
3. Click **Solve**. If the inputs are valid, the 1–9 grid fills in.

This is the same solver from the [original Suko Solver post](/2021/02/07/suko-solver/).
