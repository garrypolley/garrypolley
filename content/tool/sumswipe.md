A new letter grid every day. Drag through adjacent letters to find as many words as you want — each letter adds its alphabet value (A=1 … Z=26). The daily target is every word at 1×; reuse the same tile twice/thrice for a 2×/3× bonus (max 3×; doesn’t stack across tiles).

Swipe a word forward or backward. Keyboard: arrows, Space, Enter, Backspace, Esc. Browse previous days (up to a year back).
