A new letter grid every day. Drag through adjacent letters to find as many words as you want — each letter adds its alphabet value (A=1 … Z=26). Reuse the same tile twice for 2× or three times for 3× (max 3×; doesn’t stack across tiles). Tiers are based on that day’s maximum.

Swipe a word forward or backward. Slide back to the previous tile to undo. Browse previous days anytime.
