Project a 401(k) with annual contributions, an expected return, and optional employer match. The interesting comparison is a **plan loan**: money leaves the market, you repay it over a term at an APR you choose, and both principal and interest go back into the account to compound. The chart shows the invested balance **with** and **without** the loan. Turn on **Repeat this loan** to take another one every N years (often the same as the repayment term) and see that cycle over the full projection.

Runs in the browser. 2026 IRS elective-deferral maximums are $24,500, $32,500 with age-50 catch-up, and $35,750 for ages 60–63. Loan rules vary by plan; the usual IRS cap is the lesser of 50% of the vested balance or $50,000.
