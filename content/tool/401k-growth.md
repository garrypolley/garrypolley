Project a 401(k) with round-number steppers, then use the sliders above the chart to compare **expected return** vs **loan APR**. A plan loan leaves the market; you repay it from your paycheck, and both principal and interest go back into the account. Turn on **Repeat this loan** to take another one each time the term ends.

Runs in the browser. 2026 IRS elective-deferral maximums are $24,500, $32,500 with age-50 catch-up, and $35,750 for ages 60–63. Loan rules vary by plan; the usual IRS cap is the lesser of 50% of the vested balance or $50,000.
