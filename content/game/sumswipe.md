A new letter grid every day. Drag or click through adjacent letters to find as many words as you want — each letter adds its alphabet value (A=1 … Z=26). Your score is a 0–9 rating on a log scale (9/9 is rare). Reuse the same tile twice/thrice for a 2×/3× bonus (max 3×; doesn’t stack across tiles).

Click an adjacent letter to extend the path. Submit by releasing a swipe, double-tapping the last letter, or Submit word. Keyboard: arrows, Space, Enter, Backspace, Esc. Browse previous days (up to a year back).
