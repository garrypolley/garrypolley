A new 5×5 letter grid every day. Drag or click through adjacent open letters to form dictionary words (A=1 … Z=26). Each word **claims** its cells — later words can’t use them. Goal: claim all 25 cells. Longer words earn length bonuses; filling the board adds +15% to your score.

The word list is **ENABLE** (public domain), filtered to lengths 3–8.

Click an adjacent letter to extend the path. Submit by releasing a swipe, double-tapping the last letter, or Submit word. Keyboard: arrows, Space, Enter, Backspace, Esc. Browse previous days (up to a year back).
