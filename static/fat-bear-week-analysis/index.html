Was Backpack’s win too close to trust?

Fat Bear Week 2026 finals came down to 910 vs 89 Backpack: Backpack 103,334 — 910 98,253. Margin only 5,081 votes (~2.5% of the pair). That razor-thin finish made me suspicious — specifically that something might have been off in the voting to push Backpack over the line. Official bracket and results: explore.org/fat-bear-week.

I pulled multi-year pair totals (Wayback + live Results tables), looked at same-day gaps, turnout vs each year’s opener, and year-over-year shapes. Organizers did catch real stuffing in 2022 (~9k spam votes for Holly, discarded). On the public 2022–2026 numbers alone, though, I cannot prove fraud in the 2026 final — close races and midweek turnout dips also show up in clean-looking years.

Bottom line

Suspicion stands; proof does not — at least not from these published tallies. But 2022’s ~9k discarded spam already exceeds the 2026 margin in votes and as a share of its matchup. Given that precedent and this year’s razor-thin final, Fat Bear Week / Katmai / explore.org should recount and analyze the 2026 finals votes and publish what they find — the way they did in 2022.

What’s anomalous

Votes are not usually a coin flip. Grazer took 82.4% of the 2023 finals pair and 70.0% in 2024. Chunk won 2025 with 60.2%. Even “close” 2022 still gave 747 a 54.5% share (9.0% margin).

2026 / Backpack is the clear outlier: 51.3% share, margin only 5,081 (2.5% of pair) — thinnest final in this set. Next-closest champion share is 2022 / 747 at 54.5%.

A thin margin is suspicious-looking but not proof of fraud — it can just mean a competitive final. Still, 2026 sits alone at the bottom of the closeness scale.

2022 spam vs 2026 margin — why a recount is warranted

Documented 2022 Holly stuffing was ~9,000 fake votes (Guardian). 2026 finals margin is only 5,081 on a pair of 201,587 (~2.5%). Absolute: ~9,000 > 5,081 by ~3,919 votes (~1.8× the margin).

As a share of the matchup: ~9k was ~13.2% of the cleaned 2022 semi pair (68,370) / ~11.6% of the implied pre-discard pair (77,370). Drop that same ~9k onto the 2026 finals pair and it is still ~4.5% — larger than the 2.5% winning margin. Spam of that size would have flipped Backpack vs 910.

Given how close 2026 was, and that organizers already found and discarded stuffing once, Fat Bear Week / Katmai / explore.org should re-check and analyze the 2026 finals ballots — same integrity pass they ran in 2022 — and publish what they find. Public tallies alone still do not prove 2026 fraud; they do show the race is thin enough that known past spam would have decided it.

What the data can and can’t say

2026 finals suspicion. Backpack beat 910 by 5,081 votes. Close enough to raise an eyebrow — not close enough, by itself, to prove stuffing.

What historical patterns show. Midweek dips and finals rebounds recur. Same-day gaps usually stay modest on two-poll days. 2022 proves fraud is possible — and that cleaned Results pages won’t show the attack spike.

Waiting on organizers. Without a Fat Bear Week / Katmai / explore.org report (like 2022), public tallies alone don’t confirm 2026 fraud.
