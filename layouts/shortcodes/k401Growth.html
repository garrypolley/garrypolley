The loan comes out of the invested balance. You repay it monthly at the APR you set; principal and interest go back into the 401(k) and then earn the expected return. Cash for those payments is assumed to come from outside the plan (typically paycheck).

Typical IRS cap is the lesser of 50% of the vested balance or $50,000, repaid within 5 years (up to 15 for a primary home). 0 = take the first loan now. Repeating takes another loan of the same size on that cycle; if the interval is shorter than the term, loans can overlap.
