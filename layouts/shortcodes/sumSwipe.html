    A=1…Z=26. Adjacent includes diagonals. Tiles may be reused (max 3 visits each). Reusing the same tile twice/thrice multiplies that word by 2×/3× — multipliers do not stack across tiles. Click or drag through letters; click an adjacent tile to extend (including back onto the previous tile to reuse it, e.g. GAZA). Keyboard: arrows move, Space adds, Enter submits, Backspace undoes, Esc clears.
