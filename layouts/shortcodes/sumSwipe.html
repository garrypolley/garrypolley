      Rules

      A=1…Z=26. Adjacent includes diagonals. No reusing a cell within one word (classic Boggle). Sliding back to the previous cell undoes that step. Claimed cells stay locked. Longer words score more via length factors (4→×1.15, 5→×1.35, 6→×1.6, 7+→×1.9). Click or drag through open letters; tap a non-adjacent tile to restart. Release after a swipe to submit, double-tap the last letter, or press Submit word. Undo with Backspace or Clear. Keyboard: arrows move, Space adds, Enter submits, Backspace undoes, Esc clears.
