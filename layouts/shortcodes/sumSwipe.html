      Rules

      A=1…Z=26. Adjacent includes diagonals. No reusing a cell within one word (classic Boggle). Sliding back to the previous cell undoes that step. Claimed cells stay locked. Longer words score more via length factors (4→×1.15, 5→×1.35, 6→×1.6, 7+→×1.9). Drag or tap through open letters — a score bubble follows the path head. Release a swipe to play; tap the last letter again to confirm a tapped word. Tapping a non-adjacent letter clears the path and starts a new one. Rescue controls (Submit / Clear / Reset) are only for stuck states. Keyboard: arrows move, Space adds, Enter submits, Backspace undoes, Esc clears.
